How to Make an Effective News Story.

Assembly time: 50 minutes
Awesome rating: 100% awesome

Are you wondering how to write a news story? The steps that will be introduced will ensure maximum effectiveness and orginization. Why don't you try to make it yourself? Follow the steps and you'll make a brilliant news story!

  • Things You'll Need

  • Something to write with
  • Note taking device
  • Somewhere comfortable to write
  • Interviews
  • Water
  • Comfortable clothes

  1. Identify the topic

    Ensure that the story that you are writing is relevant. Since you are writing for news, the story needs to be timely, impactful, and have conflict close to a shared issue that people need to know about. Ensuring that you have the correct information all depends on following the 5 Ws: Who was involved with the story? What happened in the story? When did the story take place? Where did the story take place? Why did the story happen? How did the story happen?

  2. Gather Research

    Gathering information means looking for the 5 Ws. Most of this information for a story is better when the journalist is within the vicinity of the story. Proximity to a story does not always mean physically at the root of the event but can tackle issues and events that are within a community, that is proximity. Conducting interviews also helps with finding the proper information about a story. The closer to the details the easier it is to form the story correctly.

  3. Structure

    When first starting to write a news story it is important to pay attention to the structure. The structure should follow the fundamental inverted pyramid. The most important newsworthy information at the very beginning, this includes gathering the 5 Ws. Next, you want to have all the details whether it is observations from the story that you have made or quotes from interviews. The details need to be able to support the main idea of the story. Finally, the story needs to end with general information, final thoughts. The last part of the story is more so nice-to-have information.

  4. Writing Tips

    When writing a news story it is important to be unbiased upon reporting on any important event or issue. The purpose of the news story is not to make the conclusion for the audience but allowing the audience to make their own conclusions; this means to show don't tell. When looking for information, interviews are amoung the most helpful pieces of info one can collect. To ensure that you are getting the correct information from someone close to the story it is great to have some kind of way to record. To record it is better to use a phone but incase the interviee does not concent it is also just as good to have a pen and notepad. Ensure that the interviee is comfortable and concents to be recorded on any recording device. When it comes to grammar and spelling it is nessasary to re-read the story yourself or having others read it before so that you can catch all the mistakes before the final edit. Take breaks when writing, sometimes writing can be stressfull so having a sip of water or taking a break from the desk is beneficial

  5. Final Checklist.

    The final checklist requires peer review editing, making sure that the story is coherent and understandable, void of any grammatical or spelling errors. It is important when re-reading to check if the story has any ‘jargon,’ meaning the story should not have any unnecessary info. Another key aspect of writing is following the 4 Cs, which is just checking for clarity, consciousness, credibility, and creativity. It is always important to fact check all of the important key information that was collected to ensure that no one is misrepresented in the news story.
